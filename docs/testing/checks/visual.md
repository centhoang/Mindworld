# Visual checks

Lab: `/lab/visual`, keys 1-5 pick a scene, N night. Look: mosaic plane, Tile skin, decided. Lines marked for review are from the last pixel build.

- [ ] The first screen feels small, cozy and cute, and you know where to look.
- [ ] Everyone stands on the grid, and nothing looks cut off.
- [ ] People pass in front of and behind things correctly.
- [ ] You can tell each villager from the others at a glance.
- [ ] Your pet looks like yours: genes show.
- [ ] The farm reads as plots, with 4 clear stages.
- [ ] Bubbles and names are readable at normal zoom.
- [ ] Entering the house feels smooth.
- [ ] Day and night look different, and stay readable.
- [ ] Pixels stay even at any window size.
- [ ] The whole screen is one grid of cells: nothing is drawn above or below it, in every screen.
